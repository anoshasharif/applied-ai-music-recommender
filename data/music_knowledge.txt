# Study
Lofi music is commonly recommended for studying because it minimizes distractions and maintains a calm atmosphere.

Classical music is often effective for reading, writing, and concentration.

# Workout
High-energy pop music can improve motivation during exercise.

Rock music with high energy is frequently used for strength training and running.

# Relaxation
Acoustic songs help create a relaxing environment after work.

Soft jazz is often recommended for reducing stress.

# Happy Mood
Pop music with high valence and danceability is associated with happier moods.

# Sad Mood
Blues music often reflects emotional and melancholy themes.

# Driving
Electronic music with moderate tempo is commonly used for long drives.

# Sleep
Slow acoustic or classical music may help people relax before sleeping.
